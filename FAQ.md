# Frequently asked questions

### What is Arobi trying to do?

Make increasingly capable intelligence useful, productive and trustworthy enough to delegate real work to. We organize
that around three pillars, Superintelligence, Abundance and Success, with trust and assurance as the foundation under all
three. See [MISSION.md](MISSION.md).

### What can I use today?

Ullumii, in the cloud with a free plan and as a desktop app; Hue¹; Whisper; and Immaculate inside every Ullumii plan.
[PRODUCTS.md](PRODUCTS.md) gives each product's status. Everything else is labeled research or planned.

### Who is the community for?

People who build, research, deploy, test and secure AI systems every day, wherever they work. See
[BUILDERS.md](BUILDERS.md).

### Does Arobi guarantee results?

No. We do not guarantee product outcomes, employment, financial returns or any particular social or economic result.
Early-stage technology companies carry real risk, and our long-term aims are aims.

### Are the communities and labs you mention partners of Arobi?

No. Mentioning a platform, lab or community does not mean it partners with, sponsors or endorses Arobi.

### How do I follow Arobi?

Through [UPDATES.md](UPDATES.md) and [aura-genesis.org](https://aura-genesis.org). Joining a third-party community does not
subscribe you to anything from Arobi; any mailing list we run is strictly opt-in.

### Can I invest through this repository?

No. This repository does not offer securities and does not accept money, investments, reservations or commitments. See
[LEGAL.md](LEGAL.md).
