# Company

**Arobi Technology Alliance, Inc.** is a New Jersey corporation, founded and led by **Gaetano Comparcola**, Founder and
Chief Executive Officer, a superintelligence researcher.

## What we do

We build software that lets people and organizations delegate real work to capable AI and stay in charge of it: Ullumii
does the work, Immaculate checks important actions before they run and keeps the proof, and Crucible tests our own
systems to find where they fail. See [PRODUCTS.md](PRODUCTS.md) for each product and its status.

## How we make money

Software subscriptions and metered usage for Ullumii, plus enterprise licensing and deployment, integration and
implementation work, consultancy, and research and development engagements. We describe a revenue line as existing only
when it does; a planned category is not presented as revenue.

## How we work

- One team across Cloud, Desktop and research, shipping continuously.
- Every product claim tied to evidence; every public update dated.
- Builders and the communities they come from at the center of how we grow (see [BUILDERS.md](BUILDERS.md)).
