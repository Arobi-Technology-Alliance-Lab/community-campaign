# What we ship

Each Arobi product is an element of our chart, and each one has a plain status. We describe what exists today, not
what we hope to build; anything that is research or planned says so.

| Status | Means |
|---|---|
| **Available** | Released and supported for the people and workflows it names |
| **Beta** | Available to a defined audience, with stated limits |
| **Research** | Experimental; no availability claim |
| **Planned** | Intended future work, not delivered |

## Ullumii: one workspace that does the work and proves it

**Available**, in the cloud at [aura-genesis.org](https://aura-genesis.org) (every plan, including a free one) and as a
desktop app for Windows and Linux (Element 2, **Ul**, Desktop).

Ullumii is where you hand work to AI at the level of the outcome you want: research, writing, code, operations. Its
heroes and sidekicks (agents and the helpers they call on) plan and carry out the work; important actions are checked
before they run, and the record of what happened is kept for you.

## Immaculate: check AI actions before they run

**Available** inside every Ullumii plan, and to developers through the Q + Immaculate API.

Immaculate checks who is acting, what they are allowed to do and the rules you set before an important AI action runs,
and holds that action if anything is missing. It is designed for delegation at the level of a mission, not approval of
every step: routine work inside what you delegated proceeds, and a person is asked only when something genuinely crosses
a boundary.

## Crucible: we attack our own systems first

**Available** as evidence: Arobi points Crucible at its own live systems and publishes what was tested, how each check
held, and what a passing check does and does not mean.

## Hue¹ (Element 1, **Hue**, Computer)

**Available**, free to download. Hue¹ turns a PC into a calm, beautiful computer with Ullumii built in: put it on a USB
stick, sign in, and ask in plain words. Ullumii works in your apps beside you, you approve what matters, and one key
stops it.

## JAWS Browser (Element 3, **Jw**, Browser)

Ullumii's own browser for searching and opening web pages, so the work Ullumii does on the web happens in a browser built
for it.

## Whisper

**Available**: private messaging for people and teams.

## INDEMNIS

**Available**: evidence packs that give underwriters and procurement reviewers the technical evidence they ask for about
an AI system. Arobi is not an insurer and does not bind coverage.

## Research

Model orchestration, memory, agent reliability, autonomous operations and robotics assurance. These are research
directions, not product claims.
