# Builders

## Where Arobi comes from

Most of the important conversations about AI right now are not happening on stage. They are happening at 2 a.m. in
Discord threads: in the developer servers of model labs, in communities of people shipping AI apps, in open-research
collectives that train and test their own models. That is where people swap prompts and tool schemas, post the agent run
that went wrong, argue about evaluation, and help a stranger fix a bug they will never be thanked for.

Arobi's founder spent his days in those rooms, among a few dozen regulars who were there every day, building, breaking
and comparing notes across communities. The same pattern kept showing up. Agents were getting genuinely capable, and
nobody could hand them real work, because nobody could say for sure what an agent was allowed to do, what it actually
did, or how to prove it afterwards. The usual answers were either a human clicking "approve" on every step, which turns
automation back into a babysitting job, or a switch that turns the checks off, which nobody should trust with anything
that matters.

The regulars who kept pushing on that problem were the rebels of those rooms. They did not accept that the choice was
between useless safety and unsafe autonomy. Arobi is what came out of refusing that choice: delegate a mission once,
let the system work, check every consequential action automatically, and keep the proof.

## Building with us

We want Arobi to be a home for people who build with AI every day: independent developers, agent builders, researchers,
operators, security practitioners, designers, founders and curious generalists who follow hard questions across
disciplines. We want to give that work what it usually lacks: compute, research time, real products to ship into, and
a shared purpose.

How we keep that honest:

- **Relationships are explicit.** Following Arobi, joining a mailing list, talking with us in a community, contributing,
  advising, contracting and being employed are different relationships. Paid work, compensation of any kind, and any
  ownership interest exist only under written terms. Nothing here promises a job, payment, equity, tokens or bounties.
- **Communities stay their own.** We take part in independent communities; we do not speak for them. Naming a platform,
  lab or community does not mean it partners with, sponsors or endorses Arobi. We follow every community's rules and ask
  moderators before posting anything promotional. We never scrape member lists or mass-message anyone.
- **Your work stays yours until you agree otherwise.** Contributions to Arobi products happen under clear contributor
  terms. Do not send us code, ideas or data you do not have the right to share.
- **Security research is welcome, in scope.** If you find a vulnerability, report it privately (see
  [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md)); test only within written scope.

## Ways in

- **Use the products.** Ullumii has a free tier at [aura-genesis.org](https://aura-genesis.org). Build something real and
  tell us where it broke.
- **Follow the updates.** [UPDATES.md](UPDATES.md) is the dated record of what changed.
- **Report what you find.** Security issues privately, through [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

This repository does not take pull requests or issues yet; when we open them, contribution terms will come first.
